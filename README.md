<h3>About Me</h3>

I'm an AI Lead 🤖 passionate about turning cutting-edge AI into production-ready, high-impact solutions.

Since 2019, I've been deeply involved in generative AI, co-founding [StageInHome](https://stageinhome.com/) where I led the charge in revolutionizing interior design through advanced computer vision 🏠✨. I then continued this journey as ML Engineer and AI Lead at [Napptilus Tech Labs](https://napptilus.com/), and now I'm building the AI foundation at [REVEL](https://www.linkedin.com/company/revel-renting/) 🚀 — designing a centralized AI platform that enables scalable, secure, and production-ready AI across the company. My focus is bridging AI capabilities with real business systems, turning experimentation into high-impact solutions through end-to-end GenAI systems, AI strategy, governance, and reliability.

Besides work, I enjoy outdoor sports ⛰️ and socializing with friends 🍻.
